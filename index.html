Correlation ≠ Causation

The astrologer sees a correlation. The causal graph tells another story.

In this model the causal graph is fully known — because we wrote it. The Sun's light drives the circadian clock (SCN), which drives melatonin, cortisol, mood and energy. The planets contribute nothing to mood here. Yet a naive observer who only measures "the sky" versus "mood" can still find a nonzero correlation, because everything is periodic and finite samples produce spurious alignment.
